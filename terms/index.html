Terms of use

Last updated: 5 September 2026

What MamaLama is

A shared map of places that are good for feeding and changing a baby. The places and the reviews come from other parents and carers, not from us.

Using MamaLama

You need an iCloud account to contribute. You must be 13 or older. You are responsible for what you post.

Content rules

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Do not post:

  • Abuse, harassment or threats aimed at anyone.
  • Hate speech, or slurs about who someone is.
  • Sexual content, or anything involving children inappropriately.
  • Names or photographs of individual staff or other visitors.
  • Spam, advertising, or reviews of a place you have not been to.
  • Anything illegal, or content you do not have the right to post.

How this is enforced

  • Every review and suggested place is checked by a moderator before anyone else can see it.
  • Anyone can report a review. Reports are acted on within 24 hours.
  • Anyone can block another contributor and stop seeing their reviews.
  • Content that breaks these rules is removed. Accounts that post it repeatedly lose the ability to contribute, without notice.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting it you give us permission to show it in MamaLama to other users. You can delete your reviews at any time from Settings.

Accuracy

Places and reviews are contributed by users. We do not verify that a place has a changing table, that it is open, or that it is suitable. Check before you rely on it. MamaLama is provided as-is, with no warranty.
