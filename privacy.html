Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Who is responsible

MamaLama is made by Robin Elrharbi-Fleury. For anything in this policy, including a request to delete your data, contact robin.elrharbifleury@gmail.com.

What MamaLama stores

MamaLama has no server and no account of its own. It stores data in Apple CloudKit, in a public database inside the app's iCloud container. When you write something, it is stored with:

  • What you wrote — your star rating, your review text, the reason you gave when reporting a review, and the name, address, phone number, website and notes of any place you suggest.
  • A pseudonymous identifier for you — the CloudKit user record name Apple gives your iCloud account for this app. It is not your name, your email or your Apple Account. It is specific to this app and cannot be used to identify you outside it. It exists so the app can show you your own review, hide reviews you have reported, and let a moderator act on repeated abuse.

Nothing is written unless you write it. Browsing the map stores nothing.

Your location

If you allow it, MamaLama reads your location to centre the map on you and show which places are nearest. Your location is never stored, never written to CloudKit and never sent anywhere. It is used on your device and discarded. You can refuse or withdraw the permission in iOS Settings and the app still works — you just move the map yourself.

What MamaLama does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No email address, name, photo or contact list is collected.

Who can see what you write

Reviews and suggested places are public once a moderator approves them. Anyone using MamaLama can read them, alongside the pseudonymous identifier they were written under. Do not put anything in a review that you would not want to be public.

Reports are not public. Only you and a moderator can read a report you file.

Moderation

Every review and suggested place is checked by a moderator before anyone else can see it. Reported content is reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks the content rules is removed, and accounts that repeatedly post it are blocked from contributing.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete My Reviews removes every review you have written. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Places you suggested that are already on the map stay, because other people's reviews are attached to them. Email the address above to have one taken down, or to request deletion of everything associated with your identifier, including reports.

Children

MamaLama is for parents and carers. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Legal basis and your rights (UK/EU)

The lawful basis for storing what you write is the performance of a contract — you asked to publish a review. The lawful basis for keeping reports and moderation records is legitimate interest in keeping the app safe.

You have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Use the email above.

Where the data is

Apple CloudKit. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will show the new version before you next post.
