Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains what personal data the MamaLama iPhone app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and what rights you have over it. It applies to the app and to this website.

We do not collect any personal data about you through normal use of the app. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third party software in the app. The only personal data we hold is the content you choose to submit: reviews, suggested places and reports.

2. Who we are

MamaLama is built by parents, for parents. It is run by a private individual based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the purposes of the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The controller's identity is available on request using the contact address below.

Contact: robin.elrharbifleury@gmail.com

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app does not transmit:

  • analytics, usage statistics, telemetry or crash reports;
  • advertising identifiers, or any data used for advertising;
  • your name, email address, telephone number, photographs or contacts;
  • your device identifiers or IP address, beyond what is technically necessary to deliver the service;
  • any data through third party software development kits, as the app contains none.

The app has no user account and no password. Viewing the map does not create any record.

4. Data we collect

We collect personal data only when you submit content to the app.

DataWhen it is collected
Star rating and review text When you publish a review of a place
Place name, address, telephone number, website and notes When you suggest a place to be added to the map
The review reported and the reason given When you report a review
A pseudonymous user identifier Attached to each of the above

The pseudonymous user identifier is the CloudKit user record name that Apple assigns to your iCloud account for this app. It is unique to this app, is not your name, email address or Apple Account, and cannot be used to identify you outside the app. It is treated as personal data under the UK GDPR.

5. How we use your data

  • To publish your reviews and suggested places to other users of the app.
  • To show you your own contributions and allow you to delete them.
  • To review reports, remove content that breaches our terms of use, and prevent repeat abuse.

We do not use your data for profiling or automated decision-making. We do not sell, rent or share it for marketing purposes.

6. Legal basis for processing

  • Contract. Publishing the reviews and suggested places you ask us to publish, so that we can provide the service you have purchased.
  • Legitimate interests. Moderating content, handling reports and preventing abuse, in order to keep the app safe for the people using it. We have assessed that this does not override your rights, as the data involved is limited to what you have submitted and a pseudonymous identifier.

7. Content visible to others

Reviews and suggested places are published to all users of the app once approved by a moderator. Please do not include personal information about yourself, your child or anyone else in the content you submit.

Reports are not published. They are visible only to you and to a moderator, and the person reported is not told who reported them. Reported content is hidden from your view immediately and assessed within 24 hours.

You may also block another contributor so that their reviews are hidden from you. Blocks are stored on your device only, are not transmitted to us, and are lost if you reinstall the app.

8. Location data

If you grant location permission, the app uses your location to show your position on the map. Your location is processed on your device and is not stored, transmitted or recorded by us. You may refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the app will continue to work.

9. Data processors and international transfers

The app stores data in Apple CloudKit. Apple Inc. and its affiliates act as our processor for this purpose and may store and process data on servers inside and outside the United Kingdom, subject to appropriate safeguards. See Apple's privacy policy.

No other organisation receives your data.

10. Retention

Reviews and suggested places are retained until you delete them or ask us to remove them. Reports and the associated moderation records are retained for up to 12 months, so that repeated abuse can be identified, and are then deleted.

11. Deleting your data

You can delete all of your reviews at any time in the app, using Settings > Delete My Reviews. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

A place you suggested that has already been added to the map will remain, as other users' reviews may be attached to it, but it will no longer be linked to you. To request removal of a place, or deletion of all data associated with your identifier including reports, please contact us at the address in section 2.

12. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • request access to the data we hold about you;
  • request that inaccurate data be corrected;
  • request erasure of your data;
  • restrict or object to our processing of your data;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in section 2. We will respond within one month. There is no charge. As the data we hold is pseudonymous, we may ask you to make the request from the device signed in to the relevant iCloud account, as this is the only means by which we can verify that the data is yours.

13. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your personal data, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK supervisory authority: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113.

14. Children

The app is intended for parents and carers. It is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

15. Security

Data is stored in Apple CloudKit and is protected by the security measures Apple applies to that service, including encryption in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If a change materially affects your rights, we will notify you in the app before you next submit content.
